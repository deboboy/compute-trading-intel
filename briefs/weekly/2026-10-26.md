# Compute Trading Intel — Week of 2026-10-26

## Executive summary
The week's pivotal event was regulatory: the CFTC's Request for Comment on compute derivatives (docket CFTC-2026-1850) closed 2026-10-20 with 107 published comments, including formal benchmark proposals (e.g. NativX's COIL/NxCI design) — the Commission now moves from information-gathering toward a posture on compute-contract listing. The index layer is fragmenting further: a new open-source provider (Computable GPU Index, CGI) publishes independent H100/H200/B200/B300 rates, while spot-layer data shows H100 rental spreads near 4x across providers ($2.89–$11+/hr), underscoring the index-divergence risk flagged in prior weeks. On the capital layer, Blackstone publicly pushed back on AI-buildout debt fears (2026-10-21), defending GPU-collateralized neocloud lending. Still unconfirmed: CME compute futures volume/open interest (no public figures found), and ICE×Ornn futures remain unlaunched.
