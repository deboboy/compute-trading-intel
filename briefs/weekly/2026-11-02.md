# Compute Trading Intel — Week of 2026-11-02

## Executive summary
A quiet week on the derivatives layer: CME×Silicon Data compute futures remain postponed pending CFTC posture (the RFC docket CFTC-2026-1850 closed 2026-10-20; no decision published), and no new volume/open-interest figures surfaced. The capital layer provided the week's clearest new signal — Hyperbolic raised a $30M strategic round (2026-10-27/29) led by Walden Catalyst (the VC co-founded by Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan), extending the recent spot-marketplace funding run (SF Compute $40M Series A, HydraHost $100M). Public neocloud equities fell more than 30% in October, a sentiment overhang worth watching as it may pressure private-market GPU-collateral financing terms. Index layer: four competing providers (Silicon Data, Ornn, Compute Desk, SemiAnalysis) plus open-source CGI continue to diverge with no methodology changes reported this week.
