# Compute Trading Intel — Week of 2026-11-23

## Executive summary
The derivatives layer stayed frozen but the **competitive trigger came into view**. The CFTC's record for NYMEX's Silicon Data H100 (GPU1) / B200 (GPU2) compute futures still reads *Approval Pending*, with the review extended to **2027-01-08** and conditioned on "other Commission registrants not self-certifying, obtaining Commission approval, or otherwise listing comparable contracts." On **2026-11-18** Bloomberg reported **Kalshi — itself a CFTC-registered exchange — is working with index provider Compute Desk** on GPU compute futures (rental contracts plus perpetuals, no confirmed launch date or specs, still subject to regulatory requirements): the first *named* rival exchange-plus-index pairing since the extension, though not yet a self-certification or listing. ICE×Ornn, Nodal×Compute Desk and Architect's US venue remain unlaunched. The week's clearest live signal was a **first-of-kind OTC hedge**: on **2026-11-17 Architect and Luxor executed a one-year put for Hyperbolic on ~$5M of B200 rental revenue**, cash-settled monthly against Silicon Data's B200 index — compute price risk getting hedged in the market *before* any listed contract exists. The **index layer diverged again**: Silicon Data's SDH100RT **$3.10/GPU-hr** and Ornn's OCPI-H100 **$2.91** on **2026-11-22** sit ~6.5% apart, unwinding the ~1.3% convergence of 2026-11-15, while B200 softened (Ornn OCPI-B200 **$9.08**, −6.0% on the week). Capital layer: **Compute Desk raised a $45M seed led by Lightspeed (2026-11-17)**; **Akash unveiled Akash Forwards**, a physically-settled GPU-hour forward market (pilot Q1 2027); Marble launched on **$11M (Polychain)**; and Blue Owl noted neocloud unsecured bonds have widened 400–500bps while GPU-financing pricing moved only 50–75bps.
