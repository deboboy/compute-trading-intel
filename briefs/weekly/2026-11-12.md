# Compute Trading Intel — Week of 2026-11-12

## Executive summary
The derivatives timeline slipped again, and this week it slipped into 2027: on **2026-11-04 NYMEX (CME Group) voluntarily consented to a further 60-day extension of the CFTC's review** of its Silicon Data H100/B200 compute futures, moving the decision date from 2026-11-09 to **2027-01-08** — so trading now begins no earlier than early 2027 (FT, 2026-11-10/11). The CFTC's stated concerns are liquidity and the manipulation vulnerability of rental-price benchmarks, and it plans further consultation with index providers; critically, the extension is **conditioned on no other registrant self-certifying, winning approval, or listing a comparable contract** — which would void the extension and let CME move earlier. Capital layer: **Ornn is reported raising a triple-tranche round at up to ~$1B**, with Thrive Capital and General Catalyst in talks, and **Anthropic said it will not terminate its ~$41.3B Lambda compute agreement** despite Lambda missing a Texas data-center lease deadline (Lambda is pursuing up to $4B in private financing ahead of a possible 2027 IPO). Spot layer: **RunPod announced an Asia capacity expansion**, naming RUTILEA its exclusive Japan data-center-onboarding partner. Index layer: **OCPI-H100 printed $3.25/GPU-hr on 2026-11-11** while Silicon Data's H100 index sat near **$3.00 on 2026-11-08** against Ornn's **$3.37** the same day — a ~12% divergence that is exactly the settlement-reference risk the CFTC now cites as a reason to wait.
