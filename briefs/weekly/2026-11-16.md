# Compute Trading Intel — Week of 2026-11-16

## Executive summary
The derivatives calendar did not move this week: the CFTC's review of NYMEX/CME's Silicon Data H100 (GPU1) and B200 (GPU2) compute futures remains extended to **2027-01-08**, and no rival registrant self-certified, obtained approval for, or listed a comparable contract — the condition that would void the extension and let CME move earlier. ICE×Ornn, Nodal×Compute Desk and Architect's US venue also stayed unlaunched with no new filings. The week's real signal was in the **index layer, where the flagship H100 benchmarks converged and crossed**: Silicon Data's SDH100RT rose to **$3.10/GPU-hr on 2026-11-15** (7-day +9.9%) while Ornn's OCPI-H100 fell to **$3.06 on the same date** (7-day −4.08%) — the two are now within ~1.3%, down from the ~12% gap that the CFTC cited last week as its reason for waiting. On-demand **B200 stayed hot**: Ornn's OCPI-B200 printed **$9.66/GPU-hr on 2026-11-15** (≈$9.72 on 2026-11-13), +23.2% over 30 days, so the divergence has not vanished — it has migrated up the stack to the newer chip. Capital layer: **USD.AI's GPU-backed credit tokens (USDai/sUSDai) went live on the Bullish exchange on 2026-11-12**, adding secondary-market distribution to the on-chain GPU-collateral stack, and the CCIR tracker now counts **$74B of GPU-secured debt across 53 deals** (as of 2026-11-14). Spot layer was quiet — no material pricing moves or capacity deals; the front-month H100 rental band remains ~$3/hr.
